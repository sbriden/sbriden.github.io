For more than 15 years, I've helped organizations solve complex data and analytics challenges across healthcare, insurance, financial services, manufacturing, and consulting.

# My Philosophy

Technology alone doesn't transform organizations.

Successful transformation happens when business strategy, governance, architecture, and execution work together.

Every engagement starts by understanding business objectives first, then designing practical solutions that create trusted data, improve decision-making, and establish a foundation for long-term analytics and AI success.
