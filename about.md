Successful data initiatives are rarely limited by technology. More often, they are limited by strategy, alignment, governance, and execution.

I help organizations modernize their data and analytics capabilities by connecting business objectives with practical technology solutions. With more than 15 years of experience across healthcare, insurance, financial services, manufacturing, and consulting, I have led initiatives ranging from enterprise reporting modernization and cloud migrations to data lakehouse implementations, data governance programs, and analytics transformation efforts.

My approach is centered on delivering measurable business value through modern data platforms, trusted data products, and scalable operating models. Whether helping a startup establish its first data foundation or guiding a large enterprise through a multi-year transformation, the goal remains the same: enable better decisions through trusted, accessible, and well-governed data.

### Areas of Focus

- Enterprise Data Strategy
- Data & Analytics Transformation
- Data Lakehouse Architecture
- Data Product Development
- Data Governance & Operating Models
- Business Intelligence & Reporting Modernization
- Cloud Data Platforms
- AI Enablement Through Data Foundations

I believe the most successful organizations treat data not as a technical asset, but as a strategic capability that drives growth, efficiency, and innovation.
